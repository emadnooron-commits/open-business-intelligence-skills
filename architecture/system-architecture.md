The system transforms business goals into validated execution.

## Core Layers

### 1. Goal Layer

Receives strategic and operational goals.

### 2. Intelligence Layer

Analyzes the goal, context, constraints and required capabilities.

### 3. Planning Layer

Breaks the goal into objectives and executable tasks.

### 4. Scheduling Layer

Prioritizes tasks and determines execution order.

### 5. Agent Layer

Selects and coordinates specialized Agents.

### 6. Execution Layer

Runs approved workflows and tools.

### 7. Validation Layer

Checks the quality and correctness of outputs.

### 8. Memory Layer

Stores useful knowledge, decisions, results and lessons learned.

### 9. Learning Layer

Improves future planning and execution using validated feedback.

## Design Principle

Components must remain modular and replaceable.

The system should be able to replace an AI model, database, tool or service without rebuilding the entire architecture.

## Human Control

High-risk decisions require human authorization.

NOOR AI OS is designed to assist and automate operations, not to remove human leadership.
